# The Simulation

Oscar's game in Games 5 (`src/games5/the-simulation/`). The opening is built: two
people you can't see talk, the screen turns on like an old TV, and THE SIMULATION
and 666 drop in on ropes with a START button. Everything below is what START
leads into, in Oscar's words (2026-09-15), and is being built a piece at a time.

## The world

- A three-dimensional world you explore in first person: walk, look around, jump.
- You're boxed into a place. Every single block looks the same.
- Some blocks are blue. You can pick those up and carry them.
- Ladders and stairs let you get up high.

## The glitches

- There are multiple types of glitches. The moment you see one, run.
- Glitches chase you. **They cannot climb ladders, but they can climb stairs**, so
  going up a ladder is how you get safe.
- If a glitch catches you, the blocks start turning black one by one. A block that
  has gone black falls away, and you get jump scared.

## The fragments

- Glitch fragments are scattered around, about a hundred of them.
- Collecting all of them shows a screen where they're all forged together, and
  that leads to the final boss.

## The lobby, and creative mode

- Pressing START goes to a lobby: survival or creative, and how big the map is
  (small 26, medium 40, big 64, gigantic 96 — as big as a browser will take).
- Survival is the game below. Creative is an empty map with nothing chasing you,
  where you place anything yourself: blocks, blue blocks, ladders, fragments, and
  all four kinds of glitch. E places, Q takes away, 1-8 choose.
- P opens a pause menu: carry on, the glitch index, exit to the lobby, and in
  creative, play the map you built with the glitches awake.
