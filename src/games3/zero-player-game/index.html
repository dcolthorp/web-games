MACHINE WINS

Every enemy cell is gone. You never touched it.

Click the pink zone to place cells · R rotates the brush · click a matching cell to spin it · right-click erases · press Play and don't touch anything.
Portals go down two at a time: click once for the first ring, again for the other end (Esc cancels). Erasing one ring closes both. Glue is forever — glued cells never come apart and nothing can delete them.
The Smart Enemy wears a dashed halo: it runs from movers and bombs and copies itself while it runs. Corner it, or send something that keeps going.
An Arrow is a signpost — a mover that walks into one turns to follow it. Point it with R, or click it to spin it.
Scroll to zoom · WASD or the arrow keys move around · drag with the middle button or hold Shift · Fit puts the whole level back on screen.
Build anything in the Sandbox, type a name, and hit Save to keep it.
